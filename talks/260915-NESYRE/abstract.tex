% Title
Tensor networks for neuro-symbolic AI

% Abstract
While tensors appear naturally in the representation of systems with many variables, tensor network decompositions become a necessity to avoid the curse of dimensionality.
Opposed to treating tensor networks as black-box models, we show that their informed design captures the neural, logical, and probabilistic principles of artificial intelligence in a unified framework, which we call Computation-Activation Networks (CompActNets).
We further motivate this framework based on the maximum entropy principle, which selects the distribution of maximal entropy among those sharing the same characteristics, namely the mean of certain statistics.
While exponential families represent the relative interior of the mean polytope, its proper faces correspond to distributions of restricted support, connecting probabilistic and logical structure.
We exploit this connection to derive generic tensor network representations of all discrete maximum entropy distributions.
