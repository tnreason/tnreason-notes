We derive tensor network representations for any discrete distribution maximizing the entropy among those satisfying expectation constraints to statistic functions.
To this end we introduce \ComputationActivationNetworks{} (\CompActNets{}), which is a tensor network architecture expressible enough to subsume exponential families.
Oriented on the convex polytope of realizable expectation vectors we represent any maximum entropy distribution in the same architecture.
We exploit that proper faces of the convex polytope correspond with the boundary of exponential families, posing constraints on the distributions support, and derive representations of them in the \CompActNet{} architecture.
The established framework suggests ranks of tensor networks as complexity measures.
We further provide a case study of Boolean statistics, which we link to propositional formulas.
