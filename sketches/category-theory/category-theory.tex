    \begin{abstract}
        We investigate the abstractions of tensor networks by string diagrams of categories for usage in the \tnreason{} formalism.
    \end{abstract}


    \section{Semiring contractions as categories}

    Each semiring $\gensemiring=\gensemiringexp$ defines a category:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Objects are the elements of the cyclic group
        \item Morphisms are maps from the objects to $\gensemiring$
    \end{itemize}

    {\bf Open question:} Are monads the property needed for message passing?


    The contraction category on a semiring $\gensemiring$ is a symmetric monoidal category, where the functor is the tensor product.

    \subsubsection{Properties}

    \begin{lemma}
        Each contraction category on a semiring $\gensemiring$ is a cd-category.
        The copy morphism is the delta tensor on that semiring, and the discarding morphism the vector of coordinates $\srone$.
    \end{lemma}

    \begin{lemma}
        Each contraction category on a semiring $\gensemiring$ has a cap by the delta tensor seen as an effect.
    \end{lemma}

    \section{Normalization}

    Directed tensors are normalizations in the $\mathrm{Mat}$ category.
    We highlight them in the \tnreason{} formalism by directed hyperedges.

    When choosing the $\mathrm{CPM}$ functor mapping $\mathrm{Hilb}$ to $\mathrm{Quant}$, quantum states are the normalized pure density matrices and unitaries channels.


    \section{Encoding schemes as functors}

    \begin{itemize}
        \item Basis encodings, selection encodings as functors from the $\mathrm{FinSet}$ category to the $\mathrm{Mat}$ category
        \item Computation circuits and activation circuits as functors from the $\mathrm{FinSet}$ category to the $\mathrm{Hilb}$ category
    \end{itemize}

    \subsection{Basis encodings as the $\mathrm{L}_{\mathbb{B}}$ functor}

    The category $\mathrm{FinSet}$ is defined as:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Objects are finite sets
        \item Morphisms are total functions between them
    \end{itemize}

    The category $\mathrm{Mat}_{\mathbb{B}}$ of Boolean tensors is defined as:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Objects are cyclic groups
        \item Morphisms are Boolean tensors
    \end{itemize}

    The basis encoding scheme is the $\mathrm{L}_{\mathbb{B}}$ functor:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Finite sets $\arbset$ are mapped to cyclic groups $\cyclicgroupof{\cardof{\arbset}}$
        \item Functions are mapped to Boolean tensors
    \end{itemize}

    % Deterministic
    Basis encodings are always deterministic morphisms: The contraction with the copy morphism (the delta tensor) is equal to the contraction of two basis encodings with different head variables.

    % Further embeddings
    There are different functors mapping Boolean matrices to further categories.
    Functors into any contraction category on a semiring just replace the $0$ and $1$ coordinates of the morphism by the $\srzero$ and $\srone$ elements of the semiring.


    \subsection{Quantum measurement as functors}

    Computational basis measurement of circuits is a functor from quantum circuits to classical probability distributions.
    We can understand the functor based on contractions representing the Born rule.
    The measurement can be understood as a composition of the $\mathrm{CPM}$ functor with the dephasing function mapping further to $\mathrm{FinStoch}$.
