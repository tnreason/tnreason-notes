    \title{Tensor network representation of fourier transforms}

    \maketitle
    \date{\today}

    \begin{abstract}
        The Fourier transform is represented by a tensor, when unfolding the vector transform using a binary coordinate representation.
        This tensor admits a sparse decomposition into (controlled) unitary matrices.
        We derive two standard algorithms in the contraction formalism:
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Fast Fourier Transform, by layerwise contraction of the tensor network
            \item Quantum Fourier Transform, by interpreting the tensor network as a quantum circuit
        \end{itemize}
    \end{abstract}
