\chapter{Sparse Calculus}\label{cha:sparseCalculus}

\abstract{
    We here investigate schemes to efficiently represent tensors based on adapted $\cpformat$ formats.
    Optimization problems of them are related to known formats like HUBO and QUBO.
    We in this chapter develop sparse tensor representation formats based on constrained $\cpformat$ formats.
    Our motivation for these formats result from the connection to encoding mechanisms, which we have applied in previous chapters.
}
