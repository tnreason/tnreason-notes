\chapter{Mean Polytopes and Maximum Entropy Distributions}

\abstract{
    % As in paper
    Motivated by the search for maximum entropy distributions we investigate the convex polytope of mean parameters to a statistic function.
    We then show that exponential families are maximum entropy distributions to mean parameters in the relative interior of the mean polytope and generalize the result to the faces of the mean polytope.
    In the case of Boolean statistics we provide a characterization in terms of propositional logic.
    % Extensions
    Further, we investigate the mean parameter as a sufficient statistic and investigate schemes to compute the activation tensor to faces.
}
