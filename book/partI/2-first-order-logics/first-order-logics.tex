\chapter{First-Order Logic}\label{cha:firstOrderLogic}

\abstract{
    To introduce the syntax and semantics of first-order logic, we in this chapter introduce the substitution tensor structure.
    This allows to capture structured representations of systems, beyond the factored representations introduced in the first two chapters.
}
